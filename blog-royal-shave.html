The Royal Shave: What a Traditional Hot-Towel Shave Includes

Moses Executive Studio · Brickell, Miami

A traditional shave is one of the few grooming rituals that feels as good as it looks. Here is what a royal, hot-towel shave actually involves — and when it is worth booking.

More than removing stubble

A machine pass removes hair. A royal shave is a sequence designed to protect the skin and deliver the closest, most comfortable result possible. Every step has a purpose, and the whole thing is meant to be slow.

The steps

Hot towel: heat opens the pores and softens the beard so the blade meets less resistance. Pre-shave oil: a protective layer so the razor glides rather than drags. Warm lather: brushed in to lift each hair. The passes: careful strokes with the grain, re-lathered as needed for a clean, even result. Cold towel: to close the pores and calm the skin. Balm: a final soothing finish.

Who it is for

Any man who wants a genuinely close finish without the irritation of rushing it at home. It also pairs naturally with an executive haircut when you want to look fully reset in a single appointment.

Before an event

A shave is one of the best things you can book the day before a wedding, a keynote, a photo shoot or an important meeting. The skin looks calm and even, and the result photographs cleanly under any light.
